# Part I — Maximally symmetric vacua of the accepted full fermionic GB action

The local maximally symmetric background catalogue is complete for real $`g,\lambda_{\mathrm{GB}}`$ and the signed nonzero-compensator patch $`L\ne0`$. The positive-$`L`$ subcatalogue is counted separately. The sole action input is the accepted [full fermionic gauged GB density](../../../../action/gb_splus_action.md). All background equations were derived from its first-order action with arbitrary independent fluctuations.

1. [Step 1 — Inputs, fields and expansion order](STEP_01_INPUTS_AND_FIELDS.md).
2. [Step 2 — Background class, domains and arbitrary fluctuations](STEP_02_BACKGROUND_CLASSES.md).
3. [Step 3 — First-order ingredients and term coverage](STEP_03_FIRST_ORDER_INGREDIENTS.md).
4. [Step 4 — Explicit zeroth-order action](STEP_04_ZEROTH_ORDER_ACTION.md).
5. [Step 5 — First-order action, every branch, critical loci and limits](../../../background.md).

The catalogue contains the ungauged Minkowski family **GB-U**, the two simple AdS branches **GB-S$`_\pm`$**, their exceptional point **GB-E**, and every real allowed root of the additional exact degree-eight family **GB-Q**. Step 5 supplies exact real-root counts, the three further merger couplings, the three gauged flat crossings, all denominator/patch cases and the branch-by-branch limit map. This catalogue solves the bosonic background equations. The [selected AdS₅ analysis](../../../README.md) gives the supersymmetry and spectrum on that branch.

The [source-term ledger](SOURCE_TERM_LEDGER.md) accounts for all 1184 higher-derivative outer terms at the required orders. The [polynomial companion](POLYNOMIAL_CALCULATIONS.md) displays coefficient collection, Euclidean polynomial divisions, resultant factors, Sturm sequences and endpoint balances. These support the five main calculation files; they do not define a substitute nonlinear bosonic action.

The [method explanation](../support/gb-part1-background-method-EXPLAIN.md) explains the relation between symmetry, arbitrary-fluctuation stationarity and conformal descendants. Its source equations are identified alongside the derivation.

The exact polynomial divisions, Sturm sign tables and dimensional checks are displayed in the [polynomial companion](POLYNOMIAL_CALCULATIONS.md). The current [reproduction guide](../../README.md) supplies the runnable checks for the selected AdS5 branch; it does not claim to replay every branch of this catalogue.

The [Part II calculation](../spectrum/README.md) constructs the quadratic action and local spectrum on the stated GB-S+ branch, with its coupling domain and scope. The other branches in this background catalogue are not assigned spectra here.
