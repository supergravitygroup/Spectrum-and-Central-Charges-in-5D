Every nonzero coefficient pattern of the 48-component graded Euler operator is listed below. The first five columns are independent source groups; their sum is the sixth. Unlisted entries vanish in all groups. See [FC10–FC13](../../details/fermionic_reduction.md) for the covariant interpretation and restoration of general parameters.
