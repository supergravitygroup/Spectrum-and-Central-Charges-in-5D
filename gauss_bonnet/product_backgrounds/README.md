# Gauss–Bonnet on AdS₃ × Σ₂

The [supersymmetric product-background chapter](supersymmetric_ads3_sigma2/README.md)
contains the calculation details supporting the final paper's regular BPS family.
It retains symbolic couplings in the analytic bosonic reduction and explicitly
labels the finite numerical fermionic evaluations.

| Calculation | Derivation | Computational support |

is included as a component calculation used in the coupled action. Norms and
mode counts apply on the harmonic domains stated in the corresponding chapters.
