# LOG: notation across the action and spectrum

The component derivations retain their source-local symbols. The following dictionary fixes the reading correspondence.

| Common presentation | Derivation notation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| $`\beta`$ | $`\alpha`$, or $`\lambda_{\rm LOG}`$ in the component construction | Coefficient of the complete LOG invariant |
| $`L=L_0`$, $`\ell_5`$ | $`L_*`$, $`L_0`$, $`\ell_{\rm AdS}`$ | Selected compensator and AdS radius |
| $`t_F`$ | $`t=igM`$ | Internal fermion matrix in the mass equations |
| $`z`$ | Eigenvalue of $`J=t^{-1}D`$ | Dimensionless signed fermion root |
| Dirac operator | $`D=\gamma^a\bar\nabla_a`$ in the mass equations | Distinct from the composite scalar named $`D`$ in the action |

The [physical operators](ads5/details/physical_operators.md) and [fermion equations](ads5/fermionic_spectrum.md) define the source-local symbols at their point of use. The earlier R² label mentioned in the LOG branch classification is a corrected source label, not an additional invariant in this theory.
