# LOG: selected AdS5 background

[Notation dictionary](../notation.md).


Retain the total action and domain

```math
S=S_{2\partial}+\alpha S_{\log},\qquad
\alpha\ne0,\qquad g\ne0,\qquad L\ne0
\tag{SB1}
```

with real couplings and bosonic backgrounds. For the spectrum select the branch-I root that remains finite in the two-derivative limit:

```math
\boxed{
L_*=\frac{2}{1+\sqrt{1-\alpha g^2/2}},\qquad
N_*=-\frac32gL_*^2,\qquad
\ell_*^{-2}=\frac12g^2L_*^2.
}
\tag{SB2}
```

Its reality domain is

```math
\boxed{\alpha g^2\le2,\qquad \alpha\ne0,\qquad g\ne0.}
\tag{SB3}
```

No extra sign restriction on $`\alpha`$ is imposed. This selection does not invalidate the other branches. For $`\alpha g^2>2`$ this real branch-I background does not exist, but branch-II candidates retain their own acceptance criteria.

The local background equations and background Killing-spinor conditions were checked. The global prescription is the universal cover of global AdS$`_5`$, fixed sources, and reflecting boundary with zero symplectic flux. The local calculation does not specify field-dependent renormalized fermionic boundary data.

The [component input](../action/details/spectrum_input.md), [Euler equations](../action/details/field_equations.md) and [branch classification](details/background_branches.md) specify the construction. No expansion in $`\alpha`$, general auxiliary elimination, or fluctuation truncation is made.
