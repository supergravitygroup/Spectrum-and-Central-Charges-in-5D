# Gauged LOG component calculation

The calculation gives the off-shell LOG invariant through two physical fermions in the gauged dilaton-Weyl basis. Implicit fermions in supercovariant quantities are included. The independent linear compensator is distinct from the composite LOG linear multiplet.

All component derivatives are full supercovariant derivatives. Ordered differentiation precedes gauge evaluation; Q/S/K connections and the derivatives of fields whose gauge values vanish remain in the defined covariants. Gold superspace notation is converted once to the component convention.

Gold 2023 (3.24)–(3.27) defines the LOG multiplet and BF invariant. The component supplement supplies descendant expressions; Gold 2025 (1), (4), (7), (25), (27) supplies the gauged realization and gauge. Literature comparisons use the explicit conventions and boundary representative.

These documents provide the component calculation and its recorded checks. They do not provide a standalone executable assembly pipeline or an action beyond the stated fermion order.
