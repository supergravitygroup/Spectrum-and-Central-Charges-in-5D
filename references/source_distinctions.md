# Conventions used in the final calculations

The [component-to-Hessian correspondence](fermionic_input_correspondence.md)
identifies each action and its quadratic reduction. The
[common conventions](../CONVENTIONS.md) fix index positions, Clifford signs
and density normalizations.

The [Gauss–Bonnet input normalization](../gauss_bonnet/ads5/action_inputs.md)
fixes the relation of its coupling to the common invariant basis.
The [verification scope](verification_scope.md) distinguishes exact algebraic
identities from the numerical component evaluations.
