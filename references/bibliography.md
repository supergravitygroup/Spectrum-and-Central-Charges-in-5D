# Bibliography and usage map

The core entries identify the cited arXiv versions and their journal or proceedings publications. Their citation keys correspond to the [BibTeX entries](bibliography.bib), which also include the additional references cited in the detailed derivations and source extracts. Equation locators refer to the version specified at the point of use.

These references support established constructions and general methods. Coefficients in local spectrum tables are not attributed as if computed directly in these publications. Local results are traced through the [claim map](source_map.md).

<a id="CoomansOzkan2013"></a>

## CoomansOzkan2013

Frederik Coomans, Mehmet Ozkan. **An off-shell formulation for internally gauged D=5, N=2 supergravity from superconformal methods**. 2013. [arXiv:1210.4704v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.4704v3). [JHEP 01 (2013) 099](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP01(2013)099).

Gauged two-derivative off-shell construction: v3, §2 and linear-multiplet equation (2.20). Coefficients of the local field conversion are tracked separately.

<a id="OzkanPangGB2013"></a>

## OzkanPangGB2013

Mehmet Ozkan, Yi Pang. **Supersymmetric Completion of Gauss-Bonnet Combination in Five Dimensions**. 2013. [arXiv:1301.6622v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6622v4). [JHEP 03 (2013) 158](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP03(2013)158).

GB construction: v4, (5.22); Minkowski₅ spectrum in §7.2, (7.9)–(7.16). This fluxless comparison does not establish the flux-background KK spectrum.

<a id="OzkanPangR22013"></a>

## OzkanPangR22013

Mehmet Ozkan, Yi Pang. **All Off-Shell R^2 Invariants in Five Dimensional N=2 Supergravity**. 2013. [arXiv:1306.1540v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.1540v3). [JHEP 08 (2013) 042](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08(2013)042).

Component construction of off-shell curvature-squared invariants. Local gauged spectrum results require separate sources.

<a id="ButterEtAl2015"></a>

## ButterEtAl2015

Daniel Butter, Sergei M. Kuzenko, Joseph Novak, Gabriele Tartaglino-Mazzucchelli. **Conformal supergravity in five dimensions: New approach and applications**. 2015. [arXiv:1410.8682v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.8682v2). [JHEP 02 (2015) 111](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP02(2015)111).

Foundational framework for conformal superspace, action principles, and higher-derivative invariants.

<a id="GoldEtAlSuperspace2023"></a>

## GoldEtAlSuperspace2023

Gregory Gold, Jessica Hutomo, Saurish Khandelwal, Gabriele Tartaglino-Mazzucchelli. **On curvature-squared invariants of minimal five-dimensional supergravity from superspace**. 2023. [arXiv:2302.14295v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.14295v2). [Physical Review D 107 (2023) 106013](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.107.106013).

Construction of three independent four-derivative superspace invariants and the LOG multiplet.

<a id="GoldEtAlComponents2023"></a>

## GoldEtAlComponents2023

Gregory Gold, Jessica Hutomo, Saurish Khandelwal, Gabriele Tartaglino-Mazzucchelli. **Components of curvature-squared invariants of minimal supergravity in five dimensions**. Preprint: 2023, [arXiv:2311.00679v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00679v1). Published in [JHEP 07 (2024) 221](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP07%282024%29221).

v1: §3.1 BF; §3.4 gauged action; §§4.1–4.3 component multiplets; §§5.1–5.3 actions. Weyl: (5.2),(5.7); LOG: (5.12),(5.20); R²: (5.22),(5.23). Convention comparisons appear in Appendix C. Appendix letters may differ in other editions cited by local documents.

<a id="GoldEtAl2025"></a>

## GoldEtAl2025

Gregory Gold, Peng-Ju Hu, Jessica Hutomo, Saurish Khandelwal, Mehmet Ozkan, Yi Pang, Gabriele Tartaglino-Mazzucchelli. **On five-dimensional curvature squared supergravity and holography**. Preprint: 2025, [arXiv:2504.10856v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10856v1). Proceedings version: [2024 MATRIX Annals, Part I (2026), pp. 457–481](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-16202-1_21).

Recent literature on gauged Riemann-squared and GB constructions; not a source for the local mode counts.

<a id="IshibashiWald2004"></a>

## IshibashiWald2004

Akihiro Ishibashi, Robert M. Wald. **Dynamics in Non-Globally-Hyperbolic Static Spacetimes III: Anti-de Sitter Spacetime**. [Classical and Quantum Gravity 21 (2004) 2981–3014](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/21/12/012), [arXiv:hep-th/0402184](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0402184).

Relation between AdS boundary conditions and the definition of the dynamical problem; this does not solve the complete boundary problem of the present higher-derivative system.

<a id="CordovaEtAl2016"></a>

## CordovaEtAl2016

Clay Cordova, Thomas T. Dumitrescu, Kenneth Intriligator. **Multiplets of Superconformal Symmetry in Diverse Dimensions**. Preprint: 2016, [arXiv:1612.00809v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00809v1). Published in [JHEP 03 (2019) 163](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP03%282019%29163).

General framework for superconformal representations and multiplet classification; project-specific B/F root matching must be established separately.

<a id="CeresoleEtAl1999"></a>

## CeresoleEtAl1999

Anna Ceresole, Gianguido Dall'Agata, Riccardo D'Auria, Sergio Ferrara. **Spectrum of Type IIB Supergravity on AdS_5 x T^{11}: Predictions on N=1 SCFTs**. Preprint: 1999, [arXiv:hep-th/9905226](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9905226). Published in [Physical Review D 61 (2000) 066001](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.61.066001).

Supporting reference for AdS₅ representation/spectrum comparisons; the IIB compactification is not identical to this project's five-dimensional action.

## Mapping local actions to the literature

| Local object | Adopted construction source | Application in this package |
|---|---|---|
| Two-derivative gauged action | CoomansOzkan2013 | [Two-derivative action](../common/two_derivative_action/invariant.md); the [fermionic input correspondence](fermionic_input_correspondence.md) distinguishes the 2013 and 2016 maps and identifies the Hessian sources. |
| Weyl² | OzkanPangR22013; GoldEtAlComponents2023 §§4.1,5.1 | [Weyl² building block](../weyl2/action/invariant.md) |
| LOG | GoldEtAlSuperspace2023; GoldEtAlComponents2023 §§4.2,5.2 | [LOG building block](../log/action/invariant.md); the local gauged adaptation is not identified with a single published equation. |
| R² | OzkanPangR22013; GoldEtAlComponents2023 §§4.3,5.3 | [R² building block](../r2/action/invariant.md) |
| GB | OzkanPangGB2013; GoldEtAl2025 | [Local normalization and GB derivation](../common/details/action_normalizations.md); local coefficients are not assumed identical to similarly named literature symbols. |
| Complete bosonic and two-fermion representation | Relevant constructions and local composite-field maps | [Action index](../common/action_family.md) and [term provenance](two_fermion_terms.json) |

The five-dimensional N=1 terminology of Gold and collaborators is matched to the minimal theory with eight real supercharges called N=2 in this package. Field and coefficient matching is checked separately from this naming convention.

The numbered equation locators use the specified source versions. References to general methods support only the stated topics; they do not independently establish the local spectrum coefficients.
