# Calculation scope

The single-linear-multiplet map and displayed nonlinear component density
supply the action up to quadratic order in the fermions. The ordered BF code
propagates coefficients and explicit fermion degrees of the effective slots.
Implicit fermions in supercovariant connections remain part of that density.

The [current AdS5 chapter](../../two_derivative/ads5/README.md) gives the
$`12+12`$ local spectrum, the scalar at the BF bound and the stated boundary
condition. Its branch has $`g\ne0`$ and $`L>0`$. The $`g=0`$ action limit is distinct
from an AdS vacuum.
