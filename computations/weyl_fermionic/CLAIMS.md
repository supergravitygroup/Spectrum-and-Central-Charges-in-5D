# Calculation scope

The supplied component derivations give the triplet S-invariance, the M08
coefficient from Q(L), ordered Q(φ) and Clifford reductions, bivector
cancellation, and the FM map. Exact insertion into the nine BF contractions
gives 70 ungauged and 73 gauged grouped terms, with the correct $`g\to0`$ limit.

The construction retains full covariant objects through the physical fermion
order stated in the [current action](../../weyl2/action/README.md). The
[AdS5 reduction](../../weyl2/ads5/README.md) and
[paper checks](../ads5_paper_checks/README.md) give the corresponding spectrum.
