# Exact AdS5 checks used by the final paper

This package records the bounded Weyl and LOG calculations needed by
final-paper citations 11–19 and 21–22. It starts from the repository's explicit
quadratic densities and reduced equations. The checks cover the stated local operators and norm coefficients.

From the repository root, with the listed SymPy dependency available:

```sh
python3 -B computations/ads5_paper_checks/checks.py
```

The default command is read-only and prints its exact results. To refresh
only this package's two recorded output files:

```sh
python3 -B computations/ads5_paper_checks/checks.py --write-log
```

The [JSON record](outputs/verification.json) and [text log](outputs/verification.log)
are generated by this implementation. The [source manifest](source_manifest.json)
locks its local formula inputs and records the final supplied manuscript's
TeX/PDF hashes; external manuscript files are not runtime dependencies.

| Check | Starting data | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Weyl vectors | WV quadratic density, $`M(t)`$ and the undifferentiated form constraint | Matrix entries, physical determinant, eigenvectors and normalized vector/Maxwell coefficients |
| Fermion conventions and projector | Final Appendix A; definitions of $`\mathcal Q,\mathcal X`$ | Clifford/charge-conjugation identities, gamma-traceless projector and linearized gauge invariance |
| Weyl fermion coefficients | Quadratic action (18), (23), pure-$`X`$ polarization | Signed mass conversion, gravitino coefficients and both $`X`$ kinetic coefficients from action pullback |
| Critical Weyl operators | Undivided polynomials at $`\alpha g^2=-2,6`$ | Repeated factors, the $`-2`$ vector Smith form, and two separate nonzero BF scalar coefficients at $`6`$ |
| LOG roots | Coupled rows (O-43)–(O-47), cubic (O-51), paper LOGmap | Spin separation, factorization and agreement with the paper's $`\nu`$ and massless kinetic prefactor |

The arbitrary couplings remain symbolic. Exact substitutions at the two
critical Weyl values are the endpoints claimed by the paper. The Smith
calculation sets $`g=1`$ after measuring the vector operator in units of
$`g^2`$; this is a choice of units. At $`\alpha g^2=6`$, the determinant alone
is a necessary consequence, not a replacement for the coupled form
equations in the [critical analysis](../../weyl2/ads5/details/critical_branches.md).

The LOG replay verifies algebra starting from the existing direct
quadratic equations. Its input is not relabeled as a newly calculated
component-to-Hessian derivation. Simple-pole fermion coefficients use the
paper's symplectic-Majorana reality and a common healthy two-derivative
reference; critical boundary and corner contributions remain governed by
the original [norm scope](../../weyl2/ads5/details/kinetic_norms.md).
