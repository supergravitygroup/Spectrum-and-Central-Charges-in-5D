# Calculation scope

The ordered descent differentiates the full primary before applying the
physical fermion-degree projection. Standard-Weyl and gauged component data
are compared by exact rational coefficient differences. Tensor weights,
index variance, parity and canonicalization fix the displayed representation.
Seven BF contractions then give the retained density.

The [current action](../../log/action/README.md) and
[AdS5 reduction](../../log/ads5/README.md) fix the component definitions and
spectrum. [Paper checks](../ads5_paper_checks/README.md) verify the displayed
fermionic operators and roots. All-order fermionic completion is outside the
retained-order calculation.
