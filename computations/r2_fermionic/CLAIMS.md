# Calculation scope

The seven-term primed BF density and mixed linear multiplet preserve the
Majorana ordering and differentiated gauge completions. The canonical action
has 365 ordered terms, with explicit fermion degree zero or two. Its full
supercovariant objects carry the implicit fermions required at that order.
The elementary representation contains 4902 retained monomials.

The action-order derivation keeps general $`L_0>0,N_0,k`$ before choosing a
background. [Current AdS5 results](../../r2/ads5/README.md) state the spectrum,
norm domains and critical endpoints. The [exact paper checks](../r2_ads5_paper_checks/README.md)
verify the displayed regular and undivided critical reductions.
