# R² conventions

Use the [common conventions](../../CONVENTIONS.md) and the
[component definitions](derivations/component_conventions.md).
The primed composite vector and unprimed defining vector remain distinct.
The action retains physical fermion degrees zero and two, including implicit
fermions in supercovariant objects. Gauge conditions are imposed after the
covariant derivatives have acted. The [AdS5 chapter](../../r2/ads5/README.md)
fixes the branch and normalization used for the spectrum.
