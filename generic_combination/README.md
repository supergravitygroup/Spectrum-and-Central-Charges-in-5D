# General action and AdS5 central charges

The four-invariant action supplies the shared component inputs for the paper's
AdS5 central charges, massless-vector kinetic terms and anomaly calculations.
The paper calculations set the two-derivative coefficient to one and retain
all three higher-derivative couplings symbolically.

1. [Action inputs and normalization dictionary](action_inputs.md).
2. [Complete bosonic action](action.md) and [source scope](source_notes.md).
3. [Constant-curvature density and tadpoles](background_equations.md).
4. [AdS5 central charges and massless vectors](ads5/central_charges_and_massless_vectors.md).
5. [Anomalies and extremization](ads5/anomalies_and_extremization.md).

The [massless-sector calculation](../computations/generic_ads5_massless/README.md)
and [extremization calculation](../computations/central_charge_extremization/README.md)
give the source projections, symbolic results and verification commands.
Their stated domains do not constitute a full spectrum for arbitrary invariant
couplings. The individual [Weyl-squared](../weyl2/ads5/README.md),
[LOG](../log/ads5/README.md), [R²](../r2/ads5/README.md) and
[Gauss–Bonnet](../gauss_bonnet/ads5/README.md) spectra retain their own action
normalizations and branch domains.
