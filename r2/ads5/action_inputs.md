# R² on AdS5: action inputs

[Notation dictionary](../notation.md).

The theory includes the common two-derivative action plus the R² invariant. The complete [theory definition](../action/README.md) links to its bosonic density, covariant core, and explicit two-fermion terms. The [physical grading rule](../../common/fermion_order.md) includes implicit fermions in supercovariant objects.

Use the [shared conventions](../../common/conventions.md) and the [component input for this spectrum](../action/details/spectrum_input.md) together. Source expressions remain in their own declared field normalization. The common notation uses alpha, beta, and zeta for the Weyl, LOG, and R² coefficients, respectively; the detailed derivations retain their source-local symbols.

The precise source-version distinction affecting the fermion input is recorded in [provenance](source_notes.md).
