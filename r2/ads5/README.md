# R² on AdS5

[Notation dictionary](../notation.md).

The fluxless branch is specified by its scalar and auxiliary background equations. The coupled metric–scalar, vector–form and gravitino–spinor systems determine the physical roots.

The final-paper range includes $`0<\tau<2`$, excluding $`\tau=3/2`$ from the generic eigenfield formulas. The [norm discussion](details/kinetic_norms.md) includes its $`A<0`$ part, and the [endpoint calculation](details/critical_branches.md#tau-2-endpoint) uses finite matrices at $`\tau=2`$. The [exact paper-check package](../../computations/r2_ads5_paper_checks/README.md) replays the vector dualization, norm identities and the two critical points without numerical coupling choices.
