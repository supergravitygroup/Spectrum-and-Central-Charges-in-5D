# R²: notation across the action and spectrum

The component derivations retain their source-local symbols. The following dictionary fixes the reading correspondence.

| Common presentation | Derivation notation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| $`\zeta`$ | $`\lambda`$, $`\lambda_{R^2}`$ | Coefficient of the complete R² invariant |
| $`L_0`$, $`\ell_5`$ | $`L=L_0`$, $`\ell`$ in the AdS5 reduction | Background compensator and AdS radius |
| $`A_R`$, $`B_R`$, $`\nu_R`$ on AdS5 | $`A`$, $`B`$, $`\nu`$ | Coefficients defined in the AdS5 mass and norm reduction |

The source symbol $`\zeta`$ can also denote a fermion in a component derivation. Its role is fixed by the field inventory; it is not the R² coupling in that usage.
