# Common framework

The [conventions](conventions.md) and [field dictionary](fields_and_derivatives.md) define the shared notation. The [two-derivative action](two_derivative_action/README.md) is combined with the curvature-squared invariants classified in the [action family](action_family.md).

The [fermion grading](fermion_order.md) determines the component content needed on a bosonic background. The [fluctuation method](fluctuation_method.md) describes the treatment of independent fields, coupled constraints and harmonic modes. [Mass notation](spectrum_notation.md) and [kinetic norms](kinetic_terms_and_stability.md) specify how the spectra are interpreted.

Detailed identities and conversion formulae are collected in `details/`. Source-local conventions remain attached to each theory's action and spectrum.

The [verification scope](../references/verification_scope.md) separates adopted invariant constructions, analytic spectrum reductions and computational checks. The [two-derivative calculation package](../computations/two_derivative_fermionic/README.md) supplies the component-map derivation and its scoped algebraic evidence; the common construction uses the corrected lower-triplet derivative in (4.27a).
